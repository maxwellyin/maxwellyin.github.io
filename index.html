About

I am a Machine Learning Engineer with a Ph.D. in NLP, based in Toronto, Canada, specializing in large-scale LLM systems, distributed training, and production deployment.

My work spans end-to-end Transformer training, system profiling and optimization, retrieval systems, and deployment-aware evaluation under real-world compute and latency constraints.

I am particularly interested in bridging research and production by building scalable, reliable ML systems that translate modeling advances into measurable real-world impact.

Selected Work

  • LLM Systems & Scaling: Owned the design and execution of a 400M-parameter Transformer pretraining pipeline trained on 40B tokens across 8×H100 GPUs, covering distributed training, evaluation, and deployment-aware model selection.
  • System Profiling & Performance Optimization: Identified and resolved bottlenecks in distributed training, including GPU underutilization, memory pressure, and synchronization overhead, improving throughput, stability, and training efficiency.
  • Evaluation & Model Selection: Built a unified evaluation workflow integrating LM Harness and LongBench to support consistent checkpoint comparison across quality, latency, and system trade-offs.
  • Production Retrieval System: Designed a FAISS-based ANN retrieval system over 500K+ documents and 1.7M passages, reducing end-to-end latency by more than 5× while improving ranking quality.

CineSeek: Semantic Movie Retrieval

A retrieval-first semantic movie search system combining query rewriting, FAISS-based ANN retrieval, and optional LLM-based reranking and explanation for interactive natural-language search.
