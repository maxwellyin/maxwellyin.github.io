About

I am a Machine Learning Engineer / Applied Scientist based in Toronto, Canada, specializing in building scalable LLM systems from training to evaluation and deployment-aware optimization.

My recent work focuses on large-scale Transformer pretraining, distributed training optimization across multi-GPU systems, and production-oriented evaluation frameworks that balance model quality, latency, and infrastructure cost.

I am particularly interested in bridging research and real-world systems: translating modeling advances into efficient, reliable, and measurable GenAI applications.

Selected Work

  • LLM Pretraining at Scale: Trained a 400M-parameter Transformer on 40B tokens using 8×H100 GPUs with DeepSpeed, improving training stability, throughput, and memory efficiency.
  • Evaluation & Model Selection: Built a unified evaluation pipeline integrating LM Harness and LongBench, enabling consistent comparison across checkpoints on reasoning accuracy, latency, and cost-efficiency.
  • System Optimization: Identified and resolved distributed training bottlenecks, including checkpoint synchronization issues, improving multi-GPU reliability and sustained performance.
  • Retrieval System Redesign: Designed a FAISS-based ANN retrieval system over 500K+ documents and 1.7M passages, reducing end-to-end latency by more than 5× while improving ranking quality.

CineSeek: Semantic Movie Retrieval
